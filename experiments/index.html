Experiments
A collection of small experiments, prototypes and half-formed ideas, shared as I explore them.

This is where I try things out. Some experiments are visual, like effects and interface ideas. Others are code, small tools or technical explorations. They are shared as they are, usually with a live demo and a short explanation of how they work. Some will grow into something with its own place on the site, some will stay as a curiosity, and some will probably never go anywhere. That is exactly the point.
